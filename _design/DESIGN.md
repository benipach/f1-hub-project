# F1 Hub — design system

The look of every new page, decided with the owner on 19–20/11/2026 while
rebuilding the homepage section by section. It replaces the "Poster" system
(dropped halfway through development as "poco F1"; kept in
`descartado/DESIGN-poster.md` and at `/descartado/poster/`).

**The idea:** the published site's dark and its Formula 1 type, made to read
like F1's own timing graphics: data first, tables and number rows, team logos,
thin circuit drawings. Nothing that "looks made by AI": no pills, no badges,
no coloured squares, no decorative podiums.

**Reference implementation:** the homepage in Astro (`site/src/components/home/`,
`SiteNav.astro`, `SiteFooter.astro`, `layouts/Site.astro`, `styles/f1.css`).
The approved mockups are in `_design/hero-f1/` (open them through a local
server; `calendar.html` and `standings.html` are generated by `cal-gen.cjs`
and `st-gen.cjs` from the real data).

**How a section gets made:** a mockup first, the owner's changes one by one,
then Astro connected to `data/`. No page is redone twice.

---

## 1. Rules that are never broken

1. **Bold is F1 Regular at `font-weight: 700`.** Never the `Formula1-Bold` file
   (the owner prefers the browser's bold of the Regular).
2. **No extra letter-spacing.** Anywhere. The Formula 1 face is wide already.
3. **Big numbers are proportional** (countdown, clocks, points in headers): no
   `tabular-nums`, it leaves a gap around the "1". **Tables keep
   `tabular-nums`**, so their columns line up.
4. **One content width for the whole site:** `--wrap: 1440px`, centred, with
   `--gut` on the sides (32 px, 16 px on phones). No page picks its own.
5. **Circuit drawings are always the thin-border ones**
   (`img/circuits/thin/<circuitId>-layout.svg`, made by
   `scripts/thin-layouts.ts`), painted **white** through a CSS mask. Never the
   thick ones, never red, never as a faded background.
6. **Real images over invented ones:** team logos (`img/teams/<id>-logo.png`),
   tyres (`img/tyres/soft|medium|hard|inter|wet.png`), flags
   (`img/flags/<iso>.svg`, never emoji). Nothing drawn with CSS that the site
   already has as a file.
7. **Everything comes from `data/`:** names, laps, lap records, podiums,
   points. Nothing typed by hand in a component.
8. **No `title=` tooltips** (the owner's rule for the whole site).
9. **The text the owner asks for, exactly:** case included ("Race", not "RACE",
   in the timetable; "Calendar", not "CALENDAR", in the desktop navbar).

## 2. Tokens (`styles/f1.css`)

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--red` | `rgb(225, 6, 0)` | F1 red: the accent, "Live", the next round, leaders |
| `--base-900` | `rgb(6, 6, 9)` | navbar, footer |
| `--base-800` | `rgb(10, 10, 15)` | page background |
| `--base-700` | `rgb(19, 19, 28)` | hero, standings, cards |
| `--base-600` | `rgb(30, 30, 42)` | countdown boxes, empty bars |
| `--line` | `rgba(255,255,255,.08)` | row separators, card borders |
| `--rule` | `rgba(255,255,255,.25)` | separators of the number rows |
| `--text` | `rgb(220, 220, 220)` | body text, gaps in tables |
| `--dim` | `rgb(150, 150, 160)` | labels, secondary text |
| `--purple` | `#b138dd` | fastest lap |
| `--green` | `#00c853` | track status: green flag |

Tints, never full blocks of colour inside tables: the **leader** row and the
**elimination zone** in red at `.12`/`.07`, the **fastest-lap** row in purple
at `.12`.

## 3. Type

Two faces, both `woff2` in `fonts/`, preloaded by `Site.astro`:

- **`'F1 Black'`** (`Formula1-Black.woff2`): display. Section titles
  (76 px / .95, uppercase), Grand Prix names (hero 76 px, cards 20 px / 1.05),
  session names (44 px), round numbers, the big points.
- **`'F1'`** (`Formula1-Regular.woff2`): everything else, at 400 or 700.

| Role | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kicker (over a title) | 700 13 px, uppercase | in `--red` |
| Section title | F1 Black 76 px / .95, uppercase | 48 px on phones |
| Column header | 700 13 px, uppercase, white | right part in `--dim` |
| Label under a number | 11–12 px, uppercase, `--dim` | |
| Number in a row of numbers | 700 24–32 px, white | |
| Table cell | 700 15 px | names uppercase |
| Body / description | 15–18 px, `--dim` | |
| Desktop navbar link | 16 px, **not** uppercase | phone menu: F1 Black 24 px uppercase |

## 4. Layout pieces

**Section header** (`.sec-head` in `f1.css`: calendar, standings, index, driver info): kicker in red, the title in
F1 Black, and on the right either a **number row** or one line of intro.

**Number row** (`.facts`, `.now`): numbers in a row over a 1 px `--rule` line,
cells split by 1 px vertical rules, the first one without; value on top
(700, white), label under (uppercase, `--dim`). Used for circuit data, the
season count, the live numbers, the standings summary. A value can carry
meaning in its colour (`--purple` for the fastest lap, `--green` for a green
flag, `--red` for "to go"), never with an added square or dot.

**Columns with a heavy rule:** every column of content starts with a
**3 px white line** and a column header under it. A live column's line is
**red**.

**Tables** (hero top 10, standings, calendar podium):
`pos · team logo · NAME · value · extra`, 36–38 px rows, 1 px `--line`
between rows, no boxes, no column titles beyond the header line, no colour
bars. Drivers by **last name, uppercase** (hero, standings) or by **code**
(`VER`, from `drivers.json` `shortName`) where space is short (calendar
podium). Times and gaps bold.

**Cards** (calendar): `--base-700`, 1 px `--line` border, radius 10 px, four a
row (two ≤ 1100 px, one ≤ 760 px). The **next** one is solid red; cancelled
ones hatched, struck through, dimmed.

**Buttons:** red solid (main action) or ghost (1 px white at .2); radius 8 px,
700 15 px uppercase, "→" at the end.

**Live:** the word "Live" in red with an 8 px dot beating at 1.4 s (opacity
1 → .25). That's the only animation besides hovers. Never a red pill.

## 5. Sections built so far

- **Navbar** (`SiteNav.astro`): sticky, logo + "Season 2026", the sections;
  current page underlined in red; "Live" red with the dot while a session of
  the coming Grand Prix runs; on phones a three-line menu button (drawn, not
  an emoji) dropping the sections in F1 Black, plus "• Live" in sight.
- **Hero** (`home/Hero.astro`, `lib/hero.ts`): top band = round number in a
  red circle, kicker, Grand Prix name, circuit name, number row (km, turns,
  laps, lap record), thin layout on the right. Bottom band = the moment (2/3)
  and its table or the timetable (1/3). States: upcoming (countdown + timetable),
  live practice / qualifying with Q1 · Q2 · Q3 / race, results on the way,
  race result (24 h).
- **Calendar** (`home/Calendar.astro`, `lib/calendar.ts`): header with rounds,
  raced, to go; a strip with a mark per Grand Prix; a card per Grand Prix:
  round, layout, flag as tall as the name's two lines, "Sprint weekend",
  city + dates · laps · km, then the podium, the countdown or "Cancelled".
- **Standings** (`home/Standings.astro`, `lib/standings.ts`): header + number
  row; three tables of eleven: drivers P1–P11, P12–P22, constructors.
- **Index** (`home/Explore.astro`): a row per section: number, name in
  F1 Black, description, arrow; "Live timing" with the dot; "Soon" where there's
  no page yet.
- **Footer** (`SiteFooter.astro`): logo + tagline, four link columns, the
  visitor's time zone and the language, the legal notice, the credits.

**The driver profile** (`driver/DriverProfile.astro`, `lib/driver.ts`), at
`/driver-preview/<id>/`, being rebuilt section by section (the scrapped poster
profile stays at `/descartado/poster/driver/<id>/` for reference):

- **Hero** (`driver/DriverHero.astro`, `styles/driver-hero.css` on top of
  `styles/hero.css`): the homepage hero's pieces with a driver in them. Top
  band = race number in the red circle; kicker (age, code, "On the grid" or
  his years); first and last name in F1 Black; **country and team with the
  real flag and logo, big** (40–44 px, the name in 700 24 px uppercase, a
  label under it), split by a vertical rule; the career in five numbers (the
  first five that aren't zero: titles with their years, wins, podiums, poles,
  fastest laps, points, races, seasons); the full-body cutout
  (`img/drivers/<last name>.png`, transparent) head to chest on the right,
  only for the current grid. Bottom band = the season on now (or, off the
  grid, the best one he finished): position, points, rounds bar with what's
  still in play, gap to the leader (or lead over the runner-up), wins,
  podiums, poles; and his last five races as a table (result, flag, Grand
  Prix, points; wins with the red tint).
- **Driver info** (`driver/DriverInfo.astro`, `styles/driver-info.css`):
  nothing the hero shows already. Section header with seasons, teams and age
  at the debut; two columns with the heavy rule: the person (1/3, a label and
  a value per row: full name, born, died, debut with its result, every race
  number with its years, home races) and his teams (2/3, a row per stint:
  logo, name, years, races, wins, podiums, points, best; titles in red under
  the name). **This table keeps small column titles** (Years, Races…): six
  numbers in a row can't be read without them (approved 20/11). On a phone
  the numbers go to a line under the name. Before and beyond F1 under it,
  only for the drivers with those facts. A career that starts in 1990 (the
  archive's first season) says "First race on record" and has no age at the
  debut, until 1950–1989 are loaded.

## 6. What was tried and rejected

So it isn't tried again:

- Red pill "LIVE", green pill "Green flag", coloured squares in race control
  or next to statuses → plain words in colour.
- Team colour bars in tables → team logos.
- Three-letter code + name ("NOR Norris") → one or the other.
- Column titles "Gap", "Tyre" over tables → none.
- Purple text on the fastest time → the purple tint on its row.
- A podium of three cards (results on the way) and photo cards (standings) →
  the same table as everything else.
- Chequered strip → the heavy rule with "Chequered flag".
- Circuit as a faded background, circuit in red → thin layout, white, in front.
- Race control messages in the hero → left out.
- Flags in the tag line → next to the name, as tall as it.
- Five cards a row in the calendar → four.

## 7. Phones and tablets

Breakpoints at **1100 px** (two columns, smaller navbar) and **760 px** (one
column, the phone menu at 860 px). Every section is checked at 390 px with no
horizontal scroll. Number rows go two by two on phones.

## Languages

English at the root, Spanish at `/es/` (`src/i18n/`). Every word a component
shows lives in `en.ts` / `es.ts` (`heroF1`, `calendarF1`, `standingsF1`,
`explore`, `footer`, `sections`); `es.ts` has to have the same keys (the type
check fails otherwise). Neutral Spanish ("tú", "aquí"). Dates, times and
numbers through `Intl`, in the visitor's time zone.
